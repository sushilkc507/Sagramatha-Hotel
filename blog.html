A Royal Retreat at THE HIMALAYA HEAVEN

In the heart of Nepal’s vibrant capital lies a sanctuary of luxury and tradition - The HIMALAYA HEAVEN. This 5-star hotel, set against the backdrop of the majestic Himalayas, offers guests a royal retreat amidst 12 acres of picturesque gardens.

From the moment you enter, The HIMALAYA HEAVEN envelops you in its royal charm. The hotel’s 285 rooms and suites are a testament to Nepali craftsmanship, blending heritage aesthetics with modern amenities for an unparalleled stay.

Culinary delights await at the hotel’s diverse dining venues, where you can savor everything from authentic Nepali dishes to international flavors. The largest casino in the city promises thrilling evenings, while the Tranquility spa offers serene escapes for rejuvenation.

Business travelers and event planners will find their every need catered to with the hotel’s state-of-the-art meeting spaces. Whether it’s a conference or a celebration, The HIMALAYA HEAVEN ensures every event is a memorable one.

For those seeking adventure, the hotel’s prime location offers easy access to Kathmandu’s cultural attractions and shopping destinations. After a day of exploration, return to the comfort of your room, where luxury and warmth await.

Experience the pinnacle of Nepali hospitality at The HIMALAYA HEAVEN, where every stay is more than just a visit; it’s a royal experience.🏰✨

Accommodations and Recreation

Lavish and eye-aweing Design

The HIMALAYA HEAVEN Kathmandu combines luxury accommodation with diverse recreation. Guests can enjoy elegantly furnished rooms and suites, alongside a spa, fitness center, swimming pool, tennis courts, and a casino, all set within a serene, garden-like setting.
