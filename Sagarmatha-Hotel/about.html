Our Story

5★Luxury Hotel
Since 1966

The Heart of Kathmandu

Sagarmatha Hotel is a premier 5-star hotel that offers an exceptional blend of royal and traditional elegance. Set amidst 12 acres of lush green landscaped area, it boasts a magnificent view of the Himalayas.

Located in the peaceful Tahachal area, just 8 km from Tribhuvan International Airport and 4 km from the city centre — making it conveniently accessible for all travellers.

Our architecture is authentically Nepali, featuring 285 beautifully appointed heritage and modern rooms. We are home to the city's largest casino and spa, and offer wide-ranging fine dining options — from authentic Nepali and Chinese to Indian and Italian cuisines.

Sagarmatha Hotel has been a cornerstone of the Nepali hospitality industry since 1966, consistently setting the standard for service and elegance. It is naturally the preferred hotel for heads of states and dignitaries visiting Kathmandu.
