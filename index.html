Our Story

A Legacy of
Nepali Hospitality

Sagarmatha Hotel is a premier 5-star retreat set amid 12 lush acres overlooking the eternal Himalayas. Since 1966, we have been the landmark of elegance and cultural heritage in Kathmandu — the preferred residence of heads of state, dignitaries, and discerning travellers from around the world.

Our 285 beautifully appointed heritage and modern rooms blend authentic Nepali architecture with contemporary luxury, creating an atmosphere unlike anywhere else on Earth.
